About the Ritual

Sacred Vows & Eternal Love

The Varmala or Jai Mala ceremony is an ancient Vedic tradition where the bride and groom exchange garlands made of fresh flowers, often roses and jasmines, signifying their formal acceptance of one another as life partners.
